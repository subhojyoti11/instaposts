Caption
Skip forward twenty minutes in a movie, and the subtitles are still perfectly synced the instant playback resumes, because they were never actually following along with the video in the first place. Playing subtitle lines in a simple fixed sequence, one after another as time passes, works fine for a linear watch straight through, but it collapses completely the moment you jump anywhere in the timeline, since the player has no way to know which line should be showing at an arbitrary new position. Tracking total elapsed playback time has the same fundamental flaw, that running clock and the video's actual current position diverge the instant you seek, pause, or the video buffers, and once they're out of sync, every subtitle after that point is wrong too. What actually keeps subtitles correct is binding every single line directly to the video's own internal timestamp, not to elapsed real time at all. A subtitle file stores an exact start and end timestamp for every line of dialogue, defined against the media itself. The video player continuously reads its own current playback position, many times a second, and at any given instant simply looks up whichever subtitle cue's timestamp range happens to contain that exact moment. Skipping to a completely different point in the film doesn't break anything, because nothing was ever being tracked sequentially, the player just performs the identical lookup against wherever the new position happens to be, and the correct line resolves instantly. This same timestamp-cue structure underpins captions across nearly every major streaming platform, and it's also why multiple language tracks can run simultaneously without interfering, each one carries its own independent timestamp data, resolved through the exact same lookup regardless of which language is active. Subtitles were never keeping pace with the movie. They were only ever answering one question, over and over: what line belongs exactly here, right now. #streaming #softwareengineering #media #howthingswork #ravenextechnologies
